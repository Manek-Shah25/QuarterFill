# Accounting methodology

QuarterFill prefers exact standalone consolidated observations. For additive flows only,
Q2 = 6M − 3M, Q3 = 9M − 6M, and Q4 = FY − 9M. Q1 is the actual first fiscal quarter.
Inputs must share concept, unit, fiscal start and scope. Original signs are preserved;
CapEx is a positive cash payment and free cash flow is CFO minus CapEx.

Balance sheets select exact quarter-end instants. EPS, weighted-average shares, ratios
and margins are never differenced. Quarterly EPS is returned only when directly reported;
split-adjusted or dilution-sensitive EPS reconstruction is intentionally withheld.

Fiscal years are named by their annual period-end calendar year (which can differ from
the issuer's own fiscal-year label). Annual periods of 350–380 days anchor actual starts
and ends; observed cumulative boundaries support 52/53-week years. Transition periods
and nonunique boundaries require review rather than calendar interpolation.
CompanyFacts `fy` and `fp` identify filing metadata, not necessarily the observation period.

Free cash flow = CFO − property/plant/equipment payments. Net debt (long-term debt only)
= current portion of long-term debt + noncurrent long-term debt − cash and cash equivalents.
It excludes leases, commercial paper and other short-term borrowing unless those are in
the stated components. EBITDA here = operating income + cash-flow D&A, which may differ
from issuer-adjusted EBITDA and can have component scope mismatches; review is required.

An absent observation remains insufficient information. Zero requires explicit reported
zero. The app does not infer not-applicable or not-separately-disclosed from silence.
These classifications need positive documentary evidence and are not automated yet.
Conflicting versions remain ambiguous; originals and amendments are retained.
Acquisitions, discontinued operations, segment reorganizations and accounting policy
changes require analyst review. An arithmetic identity alone does not establish comparability.
